Who we are

AutoClip is a desktop application that runs on your own Windows computer. It is an independent project, not a company service, and it does not operate any server that receives your data.

Data that stays on your computer

  • Videos and edits. The videos you add, the audio extracted from them, transcripts, captions and rendered clips are stored in AutoClip's own folder on your computer.
  • Settings. Your preferences and any API credentials you enter are stored in local files in the same folder.

You can delete any project inside the app, which removes its files. Deleting the AutoClip folder removes everything.

TikTok account data

If you choose to connect a TikTok account, TikTok shares the following with AutoClip after you approve the login:

  • an access token and a refresh token, the granted scopes, and your TikTok open ID;
  • your display name and avatar URL (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.

This data is saved only in a local file on your computer. It is used for one purpose: uploading a video you explicitly choose to your TikTok inbox as a draft (scope video.upload). AutoClip does not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages or analytics, and does not post anything publicly. You add the caption and decide whether to post in the TikTok app.

To disconnect, use Disconnect in AutoClip's settings. This deletes the local login and asks TikTok to revoke the token. You can also remove AutoClip's access from your TikTok account settings at any time.

Data sent to other services

  • TikTok receives the video file you choose to send, through the official TikTok Content Posting API. TikTok handles it under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (optional). Only if you enter your own Anthropic API key and ask for AI captions, the transcript of that clip (and any product notes you type) is sent to Anthropic's API to write a hook, caption and hashtags.
  • Model downloads. The speech-recognition model is downloaded once from Hugging Face. No personal data is sent with that download.

AutoClip has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

AutoClip is not directed at children under 13, and it must be used in line with TikTok's own age requirements.

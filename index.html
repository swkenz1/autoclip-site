Short videos, edited on your own computer

AutoClip (registered on TikTok for Developers as "AutoClipSW") is a desktop app for Windows.

What it does

  • Removes long pauses and filler words from talking videos.
  • Transcribes Thai speech and burns in word-by-word captions.
  • Converts videos to vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) for short-form platforms.
  • Cleans up background noise and evens out loudness.

How it works with TikTok

  1. In AutoClip's settings, you choose Connect TikTok account and log in with TikTok (Login Kit). AutoClip asks for user.info.basic (to show which account is connected) and video.upload.
  2. On a finished clip, you press Send to TikTok. AutoClip uploads that one video with the Content Posting API (upload to inbox).
  3. TikTok sends you a notification. You open it in the TikTok app, add your caption, and decide whether to post. AutoClip never posts on its own.

All editing happens locally. There is no AutoClip server: your videos, transcripts and TikTok login stay on your computer, except for the video you choose to send to TikTok. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
